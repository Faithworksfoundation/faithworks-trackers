Who We Are

Local people, looking out for local families

Faithworks Foundation Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Berlin, Maryland. We started right here on the Eastern Shore, and this is only the beginning.

Our vision is a future where no family walks through hard times alone, and everyone knows they are seen, valued, and loved. We do that work through warm meals, hardship aid, and hope for local families.

Signups are closed for 2026

Christmas signups closed on November 8. Families on the list will hear from us by November 20, and the gift pickup celebration is December 23 at the Hilton in Ocean City.

Send us your gift list

Families we can help receive a second form for the full gift list. Each person picks two needs and one wish, so every gift is something your family actually asked for.

Who can sign up for Christmas help?

Signups for Christmas 2026 closed on November 8. The 82 families who signed up are in Worcester and Wicomico counties, and families we can help will hear from us by November 20.

Is an invite guaranteed once I sign up?

No. Signups are much bigger than our budget, with 82 families signed up so far. Families we can help get the full gift list form by email. If we cannot help your family this year, we will tell you honestly and keep you in mind if more support comes in.

What happens on December 23?

It is a celebration, not a drive through line. Come to the Hilton in Ocean City, pick up your family’s gifts, and stay for music and light food and drinks. Kids can open presents there or take everything home, your call.

How can I help a Christmas family?

Sponsor a whole family below, shop an item from our Amazon gift registry, or give through the Donate button and we will shop the gaps. If a sponsored gift gets covered, that money rolls to the next family still waiting.

Sponsor a Family

Cover Christmas for one whole family

Pick a family and cover their gifts, about 400 dollars for most families. You see first names, ages, sizes, and wish lists only. No last names, no towns, no private details, ever. If your family gets covered another way, your gift rolls to the next family still waiting, and Faithworks covers any overage. You are never asked for more.

Prayer Wall

Nobody prays alone here

Our Prayer Wall went live on October 24. It is a public board where neighbors post prayers, pray for each other, leave kind comments, and celebrate Answered prayers together.

  • Post with your name or stay Anonymous. Your choice, every time.
  • Tap the pray button so the person who posted knows they were prayed for.
  • Answered badges mark prayers God has answered, so the whole community can celebrate.
  • Every post is moderated by our team, and new prayers reach us by email right away.
