The Prayer Wall

Our online prayer wall is open to anyone: post a request, pray for a neighbor, and celebrate together when a prayer is answered.

  • Neighbors can pray along and leave comments of encouragement
  • Answered prayers get a badge — so hope is visible, not just promised
  • Visit it at faithworksfoundation.com/prayer-wall

Why a gift here goes somewhere real

It stays local

We serve families in Worcester and Wicomico Counties only. The people you help are your neighbors — you may well meet them.

It’s accounted for

A registered 501(c)(3) public charity and Maryland-registered charity (#55313). Every gift is logged in the foundation’s books.

It becomes something you can see

A seat at a table. A coat that fits. A gift under a tree. Giving here turns into a specific thing for a specific family — fast.
