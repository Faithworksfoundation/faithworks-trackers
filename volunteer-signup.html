Volunteer with Faithworks

Be the Light. Be the Hope.

Volunteers are the heartbeat of everything we do. They're the hands that serve Thanksgiving dinner, wrap gifts at our Christmas gift event, sort coats for the winter clothing drive, pack meal boxes on Mountaire packing day, and string the lights on our “Be the Light” tree at Trees for the Arts. If you've got a few hours and a willing heart, there's a place for you here — right here on Maryland's Eastern Shore.

How this works

This isn't signing up for one event — it's raising your hand so we know who to call. Simple as that.

  1. 1

    Tell us when you're free

    Pick the days and times that usually work for you, and what you'd like to help with. Two minutes, tops.

  2. 2

    We'll send the agreement

    We'll email you our short volunteer agreement to read over and sign — it just keeps everybody on the same page before you start.

  3. 3

    We reach out when there's a need

    When something comes up that fits your availability, we'll be in touch. And if you can't make one — no guilt, no pressure. Life happens.
